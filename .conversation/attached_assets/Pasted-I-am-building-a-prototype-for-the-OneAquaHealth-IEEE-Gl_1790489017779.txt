I am building a prototype for the **OneAquaHealth IEEE Global Hackathon 2026**.

## PROJECT NAME

**Aqua Quest: Water Memory**

## CORE IDEA

I do NOT want to build a generic water-awareness website, dashboard, infographic, quiz app, or water-management system.

I want to build an **actual small playable game**.

The player learns responsible water use by making decisions inside the game, experiencing consequences, remembering those consequences, and finally applying one learned behaviour in the real world.

The central gameplay loop is:

**Play → Make choices → Experience consequences → Remember → Face similar choices → Improve → Take a real-world action**

The two main concepts are:

### 1. Aqua Quest

The actual playable game.

### 2. Water Memory

The learning mechanism that makes players remember sustainable water-use behaviours through:

* repeated situations
* consequences
* recall challenges
* previous choices
* reinforcement
* progressively harder decisions

The goal is NOT to lecture the player about water conservation.

The player should **experience why sustainable choices matter.**

---

# THE MAIN GAME CONCEPT

The player starts with a limited amount of water.

They go through a short simulated day/community scenario and encounter everyday situations involving water.

Examples:

* Brushing teeth
* Taking a shower
* Washing dishes
* Washing clothes
* Watering plants
* Finding a leaking tap
* Cleaning
* Collecting rainwater
* Reusing suitable leftover water

For each situation, the player gets choices.

Example:

### Brushing teeth

**The tap is running. What do you do?**

A. Leave it running
B. Turn it off while brushing
C. Let it run slowly

The choice should have an immediate gameplay consequence.

For example:

**Wasteful choice**

* Water decreases significantly
* Score decreases
* Player receives a warning/feedback

**Sustainable choice**

* Water is preserved
* Player gains points
* A positive habit is recorded in Water Memory

Do NOT simply display “Correct!” or “Wrong!”

Instead, make the consequence visible and meaningful.

---

# WATER MEMORY SYSTEM

This is one of the most important parts of the project.

The player should encounter situations where they can demonstrate whether they remembered a previous lesson.

Example:

Early game:

> “You are brushing your teeth. What should you do?”

Player chooses:

> Turn off the tap.

Later:

> “You're brushing your teeth again. Your water supply is getting low. What do you do?”

The game should recognize/reinforce the previous sustainable behaviour.

Another possibility is a short recall challenge:

> “Earlier, you saved water by changing one habit. Which habit was it?”

The player answers.

This creates a simple memory loop:

**Experience → Remember → Recall → Apply**

Keep this system simple enough to implement reliably in an MVP.

---

# REAL-WORLD TWIST

This is extremely important.

At the end of the game, DO NOT simply show a score screen and stop.

The final stage should be:

# “Your Final Quest”

The game tells the player:

> “You learned how to save water in the game. Now try it in real life.”

Give the player a small practical action they can actually perform.

Examples:

* Turn off the tap while brushing for the next 3 days.
* Use a bucket instead of a running shower once.
* Report a leaking tap.
* Reuse suitable leftover household water for plants.
* Collect rainwater where appropriate.
* Check one tap at home for unnecessary leakage.

The player selects one action.

Then show something like:

> 💧 REAL-WORLD QUEST SELECTED
> “Turn off the tap while brushing for 3 days.”

The player can mark the action as completed.

OPTIONALLY, allow them to upload/take a photo as proof, but this should NOT be mandatory because we want the prototype to remain simple and privacy-friendly.

After completion:

> 🌍 “You didn't just learn it. You practiced it.”

Award a final badge such as:

**REAL WATER GUARDIAN**

This is the final twist of the game.

The purpose is to connect:

**Game learning → real-world behaviour**
